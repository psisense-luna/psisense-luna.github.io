01 — The Problem

Monitoring consciousness through EEG still fails

Correct signal, incorrect interpretation

Under certain dissociative anesthetics, EEG can maintain high-frequency electrical activity. Commercial monitors may interpret this activity as "wakefulness" — even when the patient is, in fact, dissociated.

This limitation has been recognized in the clinical literature for more than two decades, with direct implications for anesthesia monitoring.

No common language across states

Sleep, sedation, anesthesia, and substance-induced altered states are currently assessed through isolated metrics developed for different contexts.

There is no single instrument capable of generalizing across these distinct domains — each requires its own measurement framework.

02 — The Idea Behind the Technology

Before the algorithm, a simple question

TCR-H begins with a philosophical postulate: the continuity of organized neural activity is what sustains the continuity of consciousness in an organism. It is not about what consciousness contains at each instant — but about the substrate upon which any conscious state can exist.

Layer 1

The Theory

The philosophical hypothesis concerning what consciousness requires in order to exist. A position, not an algorithm.

Layer 2

The Instrument (TCR-H)

The engineering method that reads EEG and characterizes the neurophysiological state. Empirically validated.

Layer 3

The Index (Ψ)

A specific way of combining this reading into a single number — one of several possible representations.

Each of these three layers can be correct — or incorrect — independently of the other two.

03 — How It Works

Five dimensions, one reading

TCR-H observes the EEG signal through five different functional dimensions — without relying on any single dimension in isolation.

T

Temporal Continuity

The extent to which the signal maintains a "memory" of its own trajectory over time.

R

Meta-Representation

Activity in frontal regions associated with processes related to mental self-monitoring.

M

Temporal Memory

The ability of the signal to retain and integrate recently acquired information.

Θ

Criticality

The extent to which brain dynamics operate near a regime of maximal richness and complexity.

Ω

Environmental Coupling

The extent to which the brain is oriented toward the external environment or toward internal processing.

01

02

06 — Methodological Rigor

Designed to withstand scrutiny

Zero subject leakage

No overlap between training and test subjects, verified across all datasets.

Permutation testing

Performance remains far above what is obtained by randomly shuffling the labels.

Temporal drift control

Discrimination does not arise from the position of an epoch within the recording session.

Honest feature selection

Metric selection was performed without access to test data.

07 — Scientific Honesty

What the index does not claim

Part of TCR-H's scientific value lies in what it deliberately does not promise. This is treated as a design principle, not a footnote disclaimer.

It is not a measure of "how much" consciousness exists

The index characterizes a dynamical regime of the system — it does not quantify the amount of consciousness as though it were a thermometer.

Discrimination is not explanation

The method demonstrates that it can distinguish states with high accuracy. This does not, by itself, establish a causal relationship.

Small samples are treated accordingly

Results involving few subjects are presented as proof of concept — not as clinical validation ready for deployment.

The theory specifies what would falsify it

Each layer — philosophical, methodological, and index-level — has an explicit falsification criterion that can be openly revised.
